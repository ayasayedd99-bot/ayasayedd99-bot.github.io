Level Up Clips is a YouTube Shorts channel that shares short, credited highlights of gaming streams and Creative Commons gaming videos.

About the Level Up Clips Uploader app

The Level Up Clips Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Level Up Clips YouTube channel. It signs in with Google to:

  • upload Shorts to the Level Up Clips channel,
  • read the channel name (so it only uploads to the right channel) and search public videos (to avoid posting clips that are already everywhere),
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, % watched, likes) to choose better clips.

Every Short credits the original creator with links in its description.
