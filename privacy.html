Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers the Level Up Clips Uploader app ("the app"), a private tool used only by the owner of the Level Up Clips YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload): to post Shorts to the Level Up Clips channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to check the signed-in channel's name, check the status of its own videos, and search public YouTube videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views, average percentage watched and likes of the channel's own Shorts.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload the channel's Shorts and to choose better clips.
  • The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with, or sent to anyone else.
  • No data from other YouTube users is collected or stored.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder from the computer deletes all stored data.
